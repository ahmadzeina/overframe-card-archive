DUELIST PROFILE / 決闘者プロフィール

𝓐𝓱𝓶𝓪𝓭 𝓩𝓮𝓲𝓷𝓪

Software engineer by craft, competitive duelist by passion — curating the art and evolution of Yu-Gi-Oh! Overframe cards.

01 / ENGINEERING & CRAFT

𝒮𝑜𝒻𝓉𝓌𝒶𝓇𝑒 𝐸𝓃𝑔𝒾𝓃𝑒𝑒𝓇

I work as a Software Engineer, building clean, reliable systems with an eye for structure and detail. That same appreciation for well-engineered architecture is what drew me to catalogue Overframe cards in a tactile, physical-feeling digital archive.

Software Engineering Archive Curator
02 / COMPETITIVE DUELING

𝒪𝓃𝓁𝒾𝓃𝑒 𝒜𝓇𝑒𝓃𝒶 𝓉𝑜 𝒯𝒞𝒢 𝒯𝒶𝒷𝓁𝑒𝓈

Yu-Gi-Oh! has been one of my favorite games and anime series for years, and I still play competitively today. Most of my matches happen online — though I haven't participated in an official in-person TCG event yet, stepping onto the live tournament floor is high on my list.

Competitive Online Future TCG Competitor
03 / SIGNATURE MONSTERS

𝒯𝒽𝑒 𝒲𝒾𝓃𝑔𝑒𝒹 𝒟𝓇𝒶𝑔𝑜𝓃 𝑜𝒻 𝑅𝒶 𝒶𝓃𝒹 𝒦𝒶𝒾𝒷𝒶'𝓈 𝐿𝑒𝑔𝒶𝒸𝓎

Picking a single favorite boss monster is never easy, but The Winged Dragon of Ra holds a special place at the top. At the same time, I'm a huge Seto Kaiba fan and a dedicated Blue-Eyes enjoyer — nothing matches the presence of high-level dragons and Egyptian God cards dominating the field.

The Winged Dragon of Ra Seto Kaiba Blue-Eyes
04 / OVERFRAME VISION

𝒯𝒽𝑒 𝐵𝑒𝓈𝓉 𝒯𝒽𝒾𝓃𝑔 𝒦𝑜𝓃𝒶𝓂𝒾 𝒟𝒾𝒹 𝒶𝓃𝒹 𝒢𝑒𝓂-𝒦𝓃𝒾𝑔𝒽𝓉 𝒲𝒾𝓈𝒽

The Overframe card treatment is easily the best innovation Konami has introduced in recent years — breaking artwork past the traditional border completely changed how cards feel and look. If I could choose one archetype to receive the Overframe treatment next, it would be Gem-Knight; their crystalline fusion artwork would look unreal breaking out of the frame.
